## How to use this space

You can keep the documentation simple at first, then add depth as your project matures. A good structure usually starts with an overview, then branches into setup, usage, troubleshooting, and release history. That way, readers can find the right page without searching through unrelated content.

## What to document first

Focus on the pages that answer the most common questions: what this is, how to start, and where to look next. If you keep each page narrow and specific, readers can move quickly from one topic to the next without losing context.

<Callout kind="tip">

Write each page for one clear purpose. When a page starts to cover too many topics, split it into smaller sections or a separate page.

</Callout>
