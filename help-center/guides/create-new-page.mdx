## Add a Page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Your Project">
    Navigate to your docs project from the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Add Page'">
    Use the **Add Page** button in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name & Save">
    Enter your page title and click **Save**. Your new page appears in the navigation.
  </Step>
</Steps>
